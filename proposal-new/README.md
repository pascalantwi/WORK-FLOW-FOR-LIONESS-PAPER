# proposal-new

The user's working copy of the proposal, created 2026-11-05 at the user's request, to refine in one place what stages 1 and 2 produced.

- `proposal.md` here is a consolidated, self-contained version: the plain-language summary, the research problem and question, how the idea changed, the theory, the models, the evidence from both pilots, the full-study plan, the real-data plan, and the open decisions.
- The official proposal the workflow follows stays at the project root (`../proposal.md`). When a refinement here should govern the study, it is carried into the root proposal as a dated revision.
- `proposal.pdf` is a typeset copy of `proposal.md` (equations aligned and numbered, references in author-date form), rendered from it with pandoc and KaTeX; it is regenerated whenever `proposal.md` changes, not edited by hand.
